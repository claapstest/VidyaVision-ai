Enterprise Operations Manual

End-to-End Voice AI Calling Process

A complete visual walkthrough for admission counselors and administrators on uploading student leads, staging queues, executing autonomous AI voice calling, live call transfers, and post-call intelligence reviews.

1

Secure Counselor Sign In

Accessing the isolated workspace with role-based credentials

Each admission counselor and administrator accesses the platform via the secure VidyaVision authentication portal:

  • Enter your registered institutional work email address (e.g. counselor@vidyavision.com).
  • Input your authorized security password and click Sign In to VidyaVision.
  • Workspace Isolation: Logging in automatically activates your private staging workspace. Your draft contacts and queues are isolated to your account.
📷 Screen 1: Official VidyaVision Counselor Sign In Portal
💡 Sub-Millisecond Instant Logout: Clicking the Logout button at any time terminates the active session immediately, clearing tokens and wiping in-memory drafts to maintain data confidentiality between shifts.
2

Navigating the Executive Call Dashboard

Monitoring high-level lead readiness signals & performance metrics

Upon sign-in, the Call Dashboard delivers instant intelligence on overall calling health:

  • KPI Summary Cards: Live counters for Interested, Counselor Follow-up, Unanswered, and Connected Calls.
  • University Scope Selector: Filter all statistics by a specific institution or view combined metrics across All Universities.
  • Date Range Filter: Easily inspect results for Today, Yesterday, or custom examination windows.
  • AI Student Profile Signals: Click on extracted signals (e.g. Immediate 2026, Counsellor Req, Transferred) to isolate high-intent student leads immediately.
📷 Screen 2: Real-Time Call Intelligence Dashboard & Analytics Overview
🛡️ Data Leak Protection: Counselors without the Download Excel Sheet permission will see a locked indicator to prevent unauthorized student contact exports outside institutional databases.

Step 1 in Dialer: Importing Student Lead Lists

Uploading Excel spreadsheets (.xlsx, .csv) or pasting raw contact numbers

Navigate to the Call Dialer tab from the top navigation bar to prepare a batch calling run:

  • Mode A (Excel/CSV File Upload): Drag and drop student contact sheets. The system automatically extracts valid 10-digit Indian numbers, student names, and programs while skipping duplicates.
  • Mode B (Bulk Text Paste): Copy and paste unstructured student text from WhatsApp or email rosters.
  • Mode C (Single Number): Enter an individual student's mobile number for immediate one-off dialing.

4

Step 2 in Dialer: University Selection & Queue Staging

Assigning voice agents, setting inter-call delays, and staging contacts

Before launching calls, configure the target parameters:

  • Target University: Select the institution the Voice AI represents during the calls (e.g. VidyaVision AI Assistant).
  • Target Course: Select whether the focus is UG (Undergraduate) or PG (Postgraduate). The system automatically routes to the appropriate admission persona.
  • Inter-Call Delay: Choose breathing room (e.g. 10s, 15s, 30s) between successive calls.
  • Click Stage Contacts for Calling: Moves validated numbers into the Staged Calling Queue.
📷 Screen 4: Reviewing Validated Contacts in the Staged Calling Queue
🔒 Isolated Staging Queues: Staged contacts belong strictly to your active login. An administrator staging calls will never mix contacts with a counselor's queue.
5

Configuring Live Human Call Transfer

Enabling direct voice forwarding to counselor phones during AI calls

When prospective students express high interest or explicitly ask to speak with an officer, the AI can seamlessly bridge the call to an admission counselor's phone:

  • Toggle Enable Call Transfer to the ON position.
  • Verify the counselor's destination number with the +91 country code (e.g. +91 95730 97810).
  • Click Sync to Voice Agent: Pushes the forwarding rule live to the telephony engine. The green badge displays Live on Voice Agent.
  • Student Experience: If the student says "Can I talk to someone about fees?", the AI politely replies: "I'm transferring your call to our admission counselor. Please hold while I connect you." and rings the counselor's phone immediately.
📷 Screen 5: Direct Voice Bridge & Human Counselor Call Forwarding Settings
6

Executing & Managing Sequential Calling Campaigns

Real-time dialing monitoring, queue controls, and quiet-hours compliance

Click the green Start Sequential Calling Campaign button to begin autonomous dialing:

  • Live Progress Banner: Displays the student currently being dialed, call duration, contacts completed, and remaining queue count.
  • Interactive Queue Controls:
    • Pause Campaign: Safely halts dispatch after the ongoing conversation finishes.
    • Resume Campaign: Picks up automatically from the next uncalled number.
    • Split Queue: Divides remaining contacts or re-stages them for follow-up runs.
  • Telecom Compliance Window: Voice AI calls strictly operate between **09:30 AM and 06:30 PM IST**. If the clock reaches 6:30 PM, the campaign automatically pauses to respect student quiet hours.
📷 Screen 6: Live Campaign Progress Banner & Interactive Dialing Controls

Recent Call Logs & Lead Outcome Classification

Reviewing historical dispatches, student sentiments, and duration records

Navigate to Recent Calls to review real-time outcomes of all dialed student contacts:

  • Student Details: Name, verified contact number, and program preferences.
  • Lead Outcome Badge: Classified into Interested, Not Interested, Calls Not Answered, or Counselor Follow-up.
  • AI Feedback & Signals: Badges highlighting whether the student requested a transfer, is seeking immediate 2026 admission, or completed graduation.
  • Detail Report Button: Click to open the in-depth student review modal.

Inspecting Audio Recordings, Transcripts & WhatsApp Outreach

Verifying conversation audio, reading dialogs, and dispatching brochures

The Detail Report Modal provides 360-degree visibility into every interaction:

  • Multi-Channel Audio Playback: Listen to the student and Voice AI audio recording directly with playback speed controls.
  • Turn-by-Turn Dialog Transcript: Read the complete verbatim conversation transcript with speaker identification.
  • Counselor Notes & Follow-Up Date: Log personalized notes and assign calendar follow-up dates.
  • 1-Click WhatsApp Admission Brochure: Click Send WhatsApp Info to automatically transmit official prospectus links and application forms to the student's phone.

Team Administration & Granular Permissions (Admin Only)

Managing counselor accounts, dialing seats, and data leak protection

System Administrators manage staff accounts via the Team Management console:

  • Granular Permissions Matrix: Customize 7 individual security flags for each team member (e.g. Upload Lead Sheets, Download Excel Sheet, Start/Stop Dialing).
  • Data Leak Shield: Restrict standard counselors from exporting student contact databases to prevent unauthorized data dissemination.
  • Real-Time Account Suspension: Immediately revoke counselor access over WebSockets with zero page refresh required.
