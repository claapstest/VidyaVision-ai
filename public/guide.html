Inputting & Uploading Recipient Contacts

4 flexible options: Excel/CSV upload, Single number, Bulk paste, and Existing campaigns

Under 1. Upload calling data / recipient list, choose your preferred method to import prospective student contacts:

  • Excel / CSV Upload (Recommended): Drag and drop .xlsx, .xls, or .csv spreadsheets. The intelligent parser extracts valid 10-digit Indian numbers, cleans non-numeric symbols, pulls student names and courses, and filters duplicate entries.
  • Single Number Input: Rapid one-off dialer. Enter the student's 10-digit number and optional Recipient Name so the AI bot addresses the student personally.
  • Bulk Paste Text: Paste raw text copied from WhatsApp rosters or emails (e.g. 9876543210, Ramesh Kumar).
  • Recent Excel Uploads & Resume: Instant one-click reload of previously uploaded spreadsheets with progress badges (e.g. Resume from #201).
📷 Screen 1: Call Dialer Step 1 — Input Options (Excel/CSV Upload, Single Number, Bulk Paste, and Existing Campaigns)
💡 Multi-Sheet Appending: Click "+ Add Numbers from Excel" to append additional spreadsheets into the current batch without overwriting existing leads.

Choosing / Adding from Existing Campaigns

Re-targeting previous batches, inspecting recipient outcomes, and loading unreached leads

Click the orange "Choose from Existing Campaigns" button to access your historical campaign archive:

  • Inspect Contacts: Review individual student rows, dial outcomes, durations, and interest statuses from any completed or paused run.
  • Selective Sub-Batch Staging: Use checkboxes or range filters to select specific students who were unanswered or requested follow-up.
  • Load All into Step 2: Move past campaign contacts directly into the dialer configuration pipeline with a single click.

Step 2 in Dialer: Campaign Configuration & Voice Bot Routing

Naming the campaign, selecting target university, and automatic UG / PG bot routing

Once contacts are loaded, the "Configure Calling Settings" card activates automatically:

  • Campaign Name (Optional): Enter a custom label (e.g. MIT WPU Admissions Phase 1) or leave blank to auto-generate from the college name and today's date.
  • Select Target University: Search and pick from your institution list. The card displays campus location, website link, spoken languages (English, Hindi, Telugu), and agent ID.
  • Dynamic Voice Bot Assignment:
    • Selecting an Undergraduate program (B.Tech, BBA, BCA, B.Sc) routes automatically to 🤖 UG Admission Assistant (#270740).
    • Selecting a Postgraduate program (MBA, M.Tech, MCA) routes automatically to 🤖 PG Admission Assistant (#270604).
    • Selecting General instructs the bot to query the caller regarding their degree level.

4

Inter-Call Buffer Delay & Contact Range Selector

Configuring carrier line cooldown timers and resuming calls from specific contact numbers

Set precise timing parameters before staging numbers:

  • Step 3 Call Buffer Delay: Choose cooldown between sequential calls: 0s (Instant), 2s (Recommended Default), 3s, 5s, or 10s to give counselors breathing room to log notes.
  • Contact Range Selector: Define "From Contact #" and "To Contact #" (e.g., dial contacts #201 to #500).
  • Quick Presets: Instant buttons for All (1–N), First 100, or Resume from #N based on previous progress.
  • Excel Row Alignment: The table retains original Excel row indices (e.g. Row 202) for auditability against raw client files.

Reviewing the Staged Calling Queue Table

Inspecting validated numbers, bot IDs, and ensuring isolated user drafts

Click "Stage Contacts for Calling" to move validated leads into the active staging table:

  • Isolated Counselor Workspace: Staged drafts are private to your login session. Multiple counselors can stage their own lists without cross-contamination.
  • Full Parameter Inspection: Verify the Mobile Number, Student Name, Target College badge, Program, and Bot ID badge (#270740 UG or #270604 PG).
  • Queue Sanitation: Remove individual contacts with the red trash button, or click "Clear Queue" to start fresh.

Campaign Scheduling, Cutoff Limits & Call Transfer

Setting autonomous stop triggers and human admission counselor voice forwarding

Click "Configure" on the Schedule & Time Limits banner and set up live transfer:

  • 1. Start Timing: Select ⚡ Start Immediately or 📅 Schedule for Later Time with exact date/time picker or quick presets (In 15m, In 30m, Tomorrow 10:00 AM).
  • 2. Stop Timing (Auto-Cutoff): Choose ♾️ No Limit, ⏳ Duration Limit (e.g. 60m), 🛑 Cutoff Time (e.g. 06:30 PM), or 🏢 Business Window Compliance (09:30 AM to 06:30 PM IST).
  • Live Counselor Call Transfer: Toggle ON, enter your counselor mobile number with +91 prefix, and click "Sync to Voice Agent". When a student asks for human guidance, the call bridges directly to your phone.
📷 Screen 6: Campaign Schedule, Auto-Cutoff Limits & Counselor Call Transfer Configuration
🛡️ Quiet-Hours Compliance: Auto-cutoff halts dialing strictly when the limit is reached. All uncalled numbers remain preserved in the queue and can be resumed with one click the next morning.

Active Campaign Execution & Live Dispatch Controls

Monitoring sequential dialing, real-time progress bars, and in-progress Excel exports

Click "Start AI Calling Campaign" to begin autonomous dispatch:

  • Live Dispatch Banner: Shows the active bot persona, connected phone number, recipient name, and live call duration timer.
  • Visual Progress Bar: Real-time visual tracking showing Completed / Total Contacts (%).
  • Mid-Campaign Actions:
    • ⏸️ Pause Campaign: Safely halts dispatch after the current active call completes.
    • ▶️ Resume Campaign: Picks up immediately from the next queued contact.
    • 🔄 Stage Remaining: Pulls uncalled numbers back into Staging to adjust delay or change bot.
    • ⏹️ Stop Campaign Queue: Safely aborts the queue and saves campaign results.
  • Live In-Progress Excel Export: Click "Download Excel" during dialing to export Completed Calls Only or a Full Campaign Snapshot without interrupting the live calls!

Managing Paused Campaigns in Call Dialer

Resuming paused runs, downloading uncalled contacts, and appending new spreadsheets

Whenever a campaign is paused manually or by an auto-cutoff timer:

  • Navigation Capsule: The top header displays a glowing orange badge: ⏸️ N Paused.
  • Paused Campaigns Card: Displayed prominently at the top of the Call Dialer tab showing the exact count of uncalled leads waiting.
  • Instant Resume: Click "Resume Calling" to start dialing remaining contacts immediately.
  • Add / Append Contacts: Click "Add / Append Contacts" to load remaining numbers into Staging so you can upload an additional Excel sheet or paste more numbers to append to the campaign!
  • Excel Export: Download uncalled contacts to review offline.
