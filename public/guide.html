Part 1

Platform User Manual & Administration Guide

1.1

Secure Counselor & Administrator Sign In

Accessing the isolated workspace with role-based credentials

Each admission counselor and administrator accesses the platform via the secure VidyaVision authentication portal:

  • Work Email Address: Enter your registered corporate email (e.g. counselor@vidyavision.com or admin@vidyavision.com).
  • Password: Input your authorized password (minimum 6 characters) and click Sign In to VidyaVision.
  • Workspace Isolation: Signing in automatically loads your private in-memory draft queue. Staged calls and dialer drafts are isolated strictly to your user account.
  • Instant Logout: Clicking the Logout button at any time terminates the active session immediately, clearing tokens and wiping in-memory drafts to prevent unauthorized access.

1.2

University & Voice Bot Setup (Add, Edit, Delete)

Configuring institutional voice profiles, course catalogs, and admission agent IDs

Administrators manage the colleges and universities represented by the AI bots directly within the Call Dialer:

  • Accessing Saved Colleges: Click "Show Colleges (N)" on the Saved Colleges banner at the top of the Call Dialer tab.
  • Adding a New College: Click "+ Add New College" to open the configuration modal:
    • College Name *: Full official name (e.g., KL University Hyderabad or MIT WPU Pune).
    • Campus Location: City and state (e.g., Hyderabad, Telangana).
    • Description: Key institutional USPs used by the AI during conversation (e.g., "NAAC A++ Accredited, 100% Placement Assistance").
    • Assigned Voice Agent ID: Dedicated telephony agent identifier (defaults to 270604).
    • Supported Languages: Comma-separated list (e.g. English, Hindi, Telugu).
    • Course Offerings: Comma-separated programs (e.g. B.Tech CSE, MBA, Data Science, AI & ML). These appear in the Step 2 course dropdown.
    • Application / Website Link: URL automatically delivered to students via WhatsApp brochures.
    • Default Admission Counselor Phone: The forwarding mobile number for live call transfers.
  • Editing Colleges: Click "✏️ Edit" on any college card to update course offerings or counselor phones in real-time.
  • Deleting Colleges: Click "🗑️ Delete" with confirmation prompt to safely remove obsolete institutions.

1.3

Team Management & Granular RBAC Permissions (Admin Only)

Creating counselors, assigning roles, the 7 permission flags, and Data Leak Protection

Administrators access the Team Management portal by clicking the "Manage Team (N)" capsule in the top navigation header:

  • Executive Metrics Bar:
    • Total Team Members: Headcount of Admins and Counselors.
    • Active Dialing Seats: Staff authorized to launch live calling runs.
    • Data Leak Protection: Count of counselors shielded from downloading raw contact sheets.
    • Account Health: Operational vs suspended users.
  • Adding a New Team Member: Click "+ Add Team Member":
    • Enter Full Name, Work Email, Role (Admin or Counselor), and Temporary Password.
    • Permission Presets: Click Standard Counselor (Recommended), Senior Counselor / Queue Lead, or Full Administrator.
  • The 7 Granular Permission Flags:
    • View Reports: Access high-level analytics and KPI cards.
    • Audio & Transcripts: Listen to recordings and read transcripts.
    • Notes & Follow-ups: Log counselor feedback and send WhatsApp info.
    • Upload Lead Sheets: Import student Excel/CSV files.
    • Start/Stop Dialing: Start, pause, or cancel campaigns.
    • Manage Colleges & Bots: Add/edit colleges and agent IDs.
    • Download Excel Sheet: Export student databases (Data Leak Guard).
  • Real-Time Suspension: Toggling status to Suspended revokes access over WebSockets instantaneously without requiring a page refresh.

1.4

Call Analytics & Post-Call Interest Dashboard

Navigating KPI summary cards, university filters, readiness signals, and live Excel exports

The Call Dashboard delivers executive intelligence across all student outreach campaigns:

  • 5 Primary KPI Summary Cards:
    • Total Student Calls: Cumulative number of call dispatches.
    • Interested Students (Green): Students expressing positive admission intent.
    • Counselor Follow-up Required (Amber): Students requesting fee discussions or callbacks.
    • Not Interested (Red): Students who declined enrollment.
    • Calls Not Answered (Rose): Unreachable, busy, or switched-off phones.
  • University Scope Dropdown: Filter all analytics by a specific college or examine aggregated data across All Universities.
  • Date Range Selector: Analyze trends for Today, Yesterday, Last 7 Days, or Custom Date Picker.
  • AI Student Profile Signals: Click on extracted signals (Immediate 2026, Counsellor Req, Transferred, Grad Completed) to instantly filter the student table.
  • Download Excel Report (.xlsx): Click the green button to export full student records, durations, recordings, and lead ratings.

1.5

Recent Calls Intelligence & Counselor Actions

Inspecting call logs, multi-channel audio playback, transcripts, and 1-click WhatsApp brochure dispatch

The Recent Calls tab provides complete chronological records of every dialed call:

  • Call Table Filters: Search by student name or phone number, and filter by lead outcome or date.
  • Call Detail Modal: Click "Detail Report" on any row to open the comprehensive inspection window:
    • Multi-Channel Audio Player: Listen to the complete call audio directly in the browser with playback speed controls (1x, 1.25x, 1.5x).
    • Turn-by-Turn Transcript: Read the verbatim conversation between the AI bot and the student with exact timestamps.
    • AI Summary & Intent Badges: 2-sentence synopsis highlighting eligibility, scores, and preferences.
    • Counselor Notes & Follow-Up Date: Log personalized counseling notes and set calendar callback reminders.
    • 1-Click WhatsApp Admission Brochure: Click "Send WhatsApp Info" to automatically transmit official prospectus links and application forms to the student's mobile number.

Part 2

End-to-End Voice AI Calling Process (10 Steps)

1

Inputting & Uploading Recipient Contacts

4 flexible options: Excel/CSV upload, Single number, Bulk paste, and Existing campaigns

Under 1. Upload calling data / recipient list in Call Dialer, choose how to import contacts:

  • Excel / CSV Upload (Recommended): Drag and drop .xlsx, .xls, or .csv spreadsheets. The intelligent parser extracts valid 10-digit Indian numbers, cleans non-numeric symbols, pulls student names and courses, and filters duplicate entries.
  • Single Number Input: Rapid one-off dialer. Enter the student's 10-digit number and optional Recipient Name so the AI bot addresses the student personally.
  • Bulk Paste Text: Paste raw text copied from WhatsApp rosters or emails (e.g. 9876543210, Ramesh Kumar).
  • Recent Excel Uploads & Resume: Instant one-click reload of previously uploaded spreadsheets with progress badges (e.g. Resume from #201).
📷 Screen 1: Call Dialer Step 1 — Input Options (Excel/CSV Upload, Single Number, Bulk Paste, and Existing Campaigns)
💡 Multi-Sheet Appending: Click "+ Add Numbers from Excel" to append additional spreadsheets into the current batch without overwriting existing leads.
2

Choosing / Adding from Existing Campaigns

Re-targeting previous batches, inspecting recipient outcomes, and loading unreached leads

Click the orange "Choose from Existing Campaigns" button to access your historical campaign archive:

  • Inspect Contacts: Review individual student rows, dial outcomes, durations, and interest statuses from any completed or paused run.
  • Selective Sub-Batch Staging: Use checkboxes or range filters to select specific students who were unanswered or requested follow-up.
  • Load All into Step 2: Move past campaign contacts directly into the dialer configuration pipeline with a single click.
📷 Screen 2: Choose from Existing Campaigns Modal — Re-targeting and Sub-Batch Inspection
3

Step 2 in Dialer: Campaign Configuration & Voice Bot Routing

Naming the campaign, selecting target university, and automatic UG / PG bot routing

Once contacts are loaded, the "Configure Calling Settings" card activates automatically:

  • Campaign Name (Optional): Enter a custom label (e.g. MIT WPU Admissions Phase 1) or leave blank to auto-generate from the college name and today's date.
  • Select Target University: Search and pick from your institution list. The card displays campus location, website link, spoken languages (English, Hindi, Telugu), and agent ID.
  • Dynamic Voice Bot Assignment:
    • Selecting an Undergraduate program (B.Tech, BBA, BCA, B.Sc) routes automatically to 🤖 UG Admission Assistant (#270740).
    • Selecting a Postgraduate program (MBA, M.Tech, MCA) routes automatically to 🤖 PG Admission Assistant (#270604).
    • Selecting General instructs the bot to query the caller regarding their degree level.
📷 Screen 3: Step 2 Campaign Configuration — University Selector, Course Offerings, and Voice Bot Routing
4

Inter-Call Buffer Delay & Contact Range Selector

Configuring carrier line cooldown timers and resuming calls from specific contact numbers

Set precise timing parameters before staging numbers:

  • Step 3 Call Buffer Delay: Choose cooldown between sequential calls: 0s (Instant), 2s (Recommended Default), 3s, 5s, or 10s to give counselors breathing room to log notes.
  • Contact Range Selector: Define "From Contact #" and "To Contact #" (e.g., dial contacts #201 to #500).
  • Quick Presets: Instant buttons for All (1–N), First 100, or Resume from #N based on previous progress.
  • Excel Row Alignment: The table retains original Excel row indices (e.g. Row 202) for auditability against raw client files.
📷 Screen 4: Call Buffer Delay Cooldown & Contact Range Selector (Resume Calling)
5

Reviewing the Staged Calling Queue Table

Inspecting validated numbers, bot IDs, and ensuring isolated user drafts

Click "Stage Contacts for Calling" to move validated leads into the active staging table:

  • Isolated Counselor Workspace: Staged drafts are private to your login session. Multiple counselors can stage their own lists without cross-contamination.
  • Full Parameter Inspection: Verify the Mobile Number, Student Name, Target College badge, Program, and Bot ID badge (#270740 UG or #270604 PG).
  • Queue Sanitation: Remove individual contacts with the red trash button, or click "Clear Queue" to start fresh.
📷 Screen 5: Staged Calling Queue Table Review with Assigned Bot Personas
6

Campaign Scheduling, Cutoff Limits & Call Transfer

Setting autonomous stop triggers and human admission counselor voice forwarding

Click "Configure" on the Schedule & Time Limits banner and set up live transfer:

  • 1. Start Timing: Select ⚡ Start Immediately or 📅 Schedule for Later Time with exact date/time picker or quick presets (In 15m, In 30m, Tomorrow 10:00 AM).
  • 2. Stop Timing (Auto-Cutoff): Choose ♾️ No Limit, ⏳ Duration Limit (e.g. 60m), 🛑 Cutoff Time (e.g. 06:30 PM), or 🏢 Business Window Compliance (09:30 AM to 06:30 PM IST).
  • Live Counselor Call Transfer: Toggle ON, enter your counselor mobile number with +91 prefix, and click "Sync to Voice Agent". When a student asks for human guidance, the call bridges directly to your phone.
📷 Screen 6: Campaign Schedule, Auto-Cutoff Limits & Counselor Call Transfer Configuration
🛡️ Quiet-Hours Compliance: Auto-cutoff halts dialing strictly when the limit is reached. All uncalled numbers remain preserved in the queue and can be resumed with one click the next morning.
7

Active Campaign Execution & Live Dispatch Controls

Monitoring sequential dialing, real-time progress bars, and in-progress Excel exports

Click "Start AI Calling Campaign" to begin autonomous dispatch:

  • Live Dispatch Banner: Shows the active bot persona, connected phone number, recipient name, and live call duration timer.
  • Visual Progress Bar: Real-time visual tracking showing Completed / Total Contacts (%).
  • Mid-Campaign Actions:
    • ⏸️ Pause Campaign: Safely halts dispatch after the current active call completes.
    • ▶️ Resume Campaign: Picks up immediately from the next queued contact.
    • 🔄 Stage Remaining: Pulls uncalled numbers back into Staging to adjust delay or change bot.
    • ⏹️ Stop Campaign Queue: Safely aborts the queue and saves campaign results.
  • Live In-Progress Excel Export: Click "Download Excel" during dialing to export Completed Calls Only or a Full Campaign Snapshot without interrupting the live calls!
📷 Screen 7: Live Sequential Outbound Campaign Active Banner & Mid-Campaign Controls
8

Managing Paused Campaigns in Call Dialer

Resuming paused runs, downloading uncalled contacts, and appending new spreadsheets

Whenever a campaign is paused manually or by an auto-cutoff timer:

  • Navigation Capsule: The top header displays a glowing orange badge: ⏸️ N Paused.
  • Paused Campaigns Card: Displayed prominently at the top of the Call Dialer tab showing the exact count of uncalled leads waiting.
  • Instant Resume: Click "Resume Calling" to start dialing remaining contacts immediately.
  • Add / Append Contacts: Click "Add / Append Contacts" to load remaining numbers into Staging so you can upload an additional Excel sheet or paste more numbers to append to the campaign before resuming!
  • Excel Export: Download uncalled contacts to review offline.
